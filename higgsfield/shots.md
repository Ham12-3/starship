# Rocket film: Higgsfield shot list

Seven clips, 8 seconds each, 16:9, about 56 seconds in total.
Paste one prompt per generation. To keep the rocket looking the same in every
clip, upload the same rocket still as the reference image each time (a frame
from the Blender hero or liftoff render works).

Shared style line, already included in each prompt:
"Photoreal, cinematic, anamorphic lens, natural film grain, no on-screen text."

## 1. Factory

A tall stainless-steel two-stage rocket standing upright inside a vast
assembly hangar at night. Welders throw sparks at the base, overhead cranes
lower a nose section into place, technicians in white suits walk past on
catwalks. Slow crane shot rising from the floor up the full height of the
rocket. Warm sodium work lights against cool steel. Photoreal, cinematic,
anamorphic lens, natural film grain, no on-screen text.

Narration: "Every journey to the stars begins on the factory floor."

## 2. Rollout to the pad

The finished stainless-steel rocket rides upright on a huge crawler
transporter along a road toward a coastal launch tower at dawn. Mist over the
ground, pink and gold sky, tiny support vehicles alongside for scale. Wide
tracking shot moving with the rocket. Photoreal, cinematic, anamorphic lens,
natural film grain, no on-screen text.

Narration: "Piece by piece it is built, then rolled out to meet the sky."

## 3. On the pad, about to launch

The stainless-steel rocket stands on the launch mount beside the tower just
before liftoff. White vapour vents from its sides, frost on the tanks, the
tower arms pull back, floodlights cut through the haze. Slow push-in from a
low angle looking up. Photoreal, cinematic, anamorphic lens, natural film
grain, no on-screen text.

Narration: "Fuelled, checked, and counting down."

## 4. Engines from underneath

View from directly beneath the rocket looking straight up at a ring of dozens
of large engine bells. They ignite in sequence, blue-white flame with shock
diamonds fills the frame, a deluge of water turns to steam, the camera shakes.
Locked-off extreme low angle. Photoreal, cinematic, anamorphic lens, natural
film grain, no on-screen text.

Narration: "Thirty-three engines wake at once, more thrust than any machine before it."

## 5. Liftoff and climb

The stainless-steel rocket lifts off the pad on a pillar of fire and climbs
through thin cloud, a long bright exhaust trail behind it, the coastline
shrinking below. Camera tilts up to follow as the blue sky darkens toward
black. Photoreal, cinematic, anamorphic lens, natural film grain, no on-screen
text.

Narration: "It leaves the ground and climbs through the last of the air."

## 6. Space, the galaxy and the planets

The upper stage of the stainless-steel rocket coasts silently in orbit above
the curved blue Earth. The camera slowly drifts past it to reveal the bright
band of the Milky Way, then distant planets in a line: red Mars, banded
Jupiter, ringed Saturn. Slow, weightless camera move. Photoreal, cinematic,
anamorphic lens, natural film grain, no on-screen text.

Narration: "Beyond lie our neighbouring planets, and a galaxy of hundreds of billions of stars."

## 7. Satellite deployment

In orbit above Earth, a slot on the side of the stainless-steel upper stage
opens and a row of flat satellites slides out one after another. Each unfolds
a solar panel that catches the sunlight as it drifts away in a neat line over
the planet. Slow orbiting camera, sunrise on the horizon. Photoreal,
cinematic, anamorphic lens, natural film grain, no on-screen text.

Narration: "Its work begins here, setting satellites into orbit one by one."
